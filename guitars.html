Handmade Guitars

Xephos and Zealot


Xephos

Like all guitar players, my hunger for a guitar collection consumes me. However, I do not want to pour 1000 dollars a new cool guitar, so I decided to make one instead. I really like the sharp look of guitars that gives it a very metal feeling, so I gave the guitar pointy ends. The design was inspired by the Jackson Warrior Extreme, and I decided to give it a more uniform X shape.

I coated the body with a Tru Oil finish, because the natural wood look supplements the maple neck I salvaged from a cheap jackson guitar. The neck pickup is an EMG H4 for its bright tone. I decided to use only a neck pickup because that is the most metal configuration. The bridge is a hard tail so I can use alternate tuning for heavy riffs.


Zealot

The Zealot is my second hand built guitar. I wanted to travel with the guitar more easily, so I made designed the body to fit into a standard stratocaster case. However, the strat shape is too lame for my taste and I designed the Zealot to look nastier and aggressive. The craftsmanship on the Zealot is much higher quality, because I used a new bandsaw and filing set. Moreover, I decided to include the electronics using a pick guard to allow me to make changes to the electronics in the future.

Similar to the Xephos, I stripped the neck and some gold hardware from another cheap Jackson guitar. I coated the body with a Tru Oil finish, because the natural wood look and long grains look astonishing with the gold hardware. I decided to use a Fishman Fluence Modern pickup because I am able to use various tone voices and coil tap to make my playing more versatile.
