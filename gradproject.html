Graduate Research Project (Work in Progress)

Benchmarking of AI Architectures


Systolic Array IP RTL


Introduction

The purpose of this research project is to benchmark different processing architectures for AI related applications. Systolic exeuction, the Strassen Algorithm, and other potential methods will be tested for performance, area, and other characteristics. A variety of matrix data test sets will be used to cover a wide range of application to show where each computation method is optimal. The implementation will be uploaded to a Zedboard Zynq system for testing.
